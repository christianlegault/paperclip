## Plan it in winter, ride it in May

Group trips fall apart on dates, not on routes. The Pinewater Highlands Traverse is
at its best for about five weeks in late spring, after the high passes melt out and
before the June heat. If you want your crew there, pick dates now.

## Planning with a crew

- The night 2 permit is per group, max 6. Book it the day reservations open.
- The shuttle back to Alder Falls runs on weekends only, so plan a Friday–Monday trip.

## Also consider

- **Lowland Lakes Loop** (2 days, 120 km): the same region, with no permits and a
  good choice for a first group overnighter.
- **Ridge Road Out-and-Back** (3 days, 170 km): more climbing, fewer services, for a
  fast crew.
