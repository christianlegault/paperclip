## A long weekend your whole crew can actually finish

The Cedar Hollow Loop is a three-day gravel circuit that suits a mixed group: the
climbing comes early each day, the camps are on lakes, and there's a café or store
roughly every 40 km. Late September through mid-October is the sweet spot, after
the bugs and before the first frost on the high road. Start and finish in Millbrook,
where you can leave cars for free at the community centre.

## Stage by stage

### Day 1: Millbrook → Heron Lake · 64 km / 40 mi · +980 m / +3,220 ft

The day opens with the loop's biggest climb, a steady 9 km gravel grade. Get it done
while the legs are fresh. After the ridge, it's rolling forest road down to the lake.

- **Camp:** Heron Lake (frontcountry, first-come, 12 sites)
- **Resupply:** Ridgeway General Store at km 38
- **Water:** Lake filter at camp; creek at km 22 (seasonal)
- **Bail-out:** Paved Hwy 9 back to Millbrook from km 38 (24 km)

## Planning with a crew

- Heron Lake's group site holds up to 8 riders. Above that, split across two sites.
- The Ridgeway store (km 38, Day 1) is the natural regroup point. Faster riders can
  wait there instead of on the climb.
- A slower rider can skip Day 2's ridge spur and take the valley road, which saves
  14 km and 400 m.
