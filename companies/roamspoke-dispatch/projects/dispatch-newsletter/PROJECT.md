Home for every Dispatch issue, the Dispatch Ledger, and newsletter routines.

**Plugs into RoamSpoke Base Camp.** The newsletter is a standalone content stream.
Its product touchpoints (a Base Camp note per issue with the route tagged, a signup
and archive surface, and a copy-trip deep link that opens a shared trip and its
invite flow) are requested from the RoamSpoke product team through the CEO. This
project never changes product code.

**Approval gate:** the board approves and sends every issue. No agent has send permissions.
